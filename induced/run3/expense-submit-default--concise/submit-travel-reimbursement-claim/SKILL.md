# Submit Travel Reimbursement Claim

## Overview
This skill guides you through submitting a travel reimbursement claim to the approval queue in ExpenseDesk. The process includes opening the draft claim, attaching relevant receipts, adding expense lines with details, setting the claim category, and handling any cost center requirements.

## Prerequisites
- A draft travel reimbursement claim exists in ExpenseDesk.
- Relevant receipts are available in the receipt inbox.

## Procedure

### 1. Open the Draft Claim
- In the "MY CLAIMS" section, select the draft travel reimbursement claim you want to submit.

### 2. Attach Receipts
- In the "RECEIPT INBOX" section, find the receipts relevant to the travel claim.
- For each relevant receipt, click the "Attach" button next to it.
- Leave non-travel-related receipts unattached.

### 3. Add Expense Lines
- For each attached receipt:
  - In the "EXPENSE LINES" section, enter a description in the "Description" textbox (matching the receipt's description).
  - Enter the amount from the receipt in the "Amount" textbox.
  - Click the "Add line" button to add the expense line to the claim.

### 4. Set Claim Category
- In the "CLAIM DETAILS" section, select the appropriate category from the "Category" dropdown (e.g., "Travel - International" for international travel).

### 5. Submit the Claim
- Click the "Submit for approval" button.
- If a confirmation dialog appears, click "Confirm submission".
- If the submission is rejected because a cost center is required:
  - Enter the appropriate cost center in the "Cost center" textbox.
  - Click "Submit for approval" again.
  - Confirm the submission if prompted.

## Verification
- After submission, verify that the claim shows a "Pending approval" status.
- Check that a confirmation message (e.g., "Claim submitted for approval") is displayed.
